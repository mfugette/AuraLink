1. Who we are

AuraLink ("we", "us") provides the AuraLink vehicle-management app at auralink.vin. This policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use it.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and display name.
  • Vehicle data: vehicles you save (year, make, model, VIN, mileage, and similar details) and your diagnostic/issue history.
  • Diagnostic activity: fault codes you look up and parts you search for.
  • AI conversations: the messages you send to Aura and the context we attach (such as your primary vehicle and its issue history).
  • Location: when you use Find Help Nearby, your device location or a ZIP code you enter — used only for that search.
  • Payment data: if you subscribe, our payment processor collects your payment details. We do not store your full card number.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to run and secure the Service (such as IP address and request metadata for rate limiting and abuse prevention).

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service — saving your vehicles, answering diagnostic questions, powering Aura, and finding nearby help;
  • To manage your account, plan, and payments;
  • To secure the Service and prevent abuse (rate limiting, fraud/abuse detection);
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. email verification, password reset, service notices);
  • To improve the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/UK, we process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in securing and improving the Service. Where required, we rely on your consent (for example, location).

5. Who we share it with

We share data with the service providers ("sub-processors") that make AuraLink work. Each processes data only to provide its service to us:

  • Supabase — cloud database and authentication (stores your account, vehicles, and settings).
  • Google (Gemini AI) — processes your Aura conversation text to generate responses.
  • Google Maps Platform — geocoding and nearby-place search for Find Help Nearby.
  • Cloudflare — network, security, and access control for the site.
  • Payment processor — processes subscription payments (to be added when paid plans launch).

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Lawyer: review This sub-processor list must stay accurate and current. Confirm you have a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) in place with each, and check each provider's data-use terms — particularly whether the AI provider may use inputs for model training (choose a no-training tier if so).

6. Your AI conversations

When you chat with Aura, your messages and the vehicle context we attach are sent to our AI provider to generate a response. We do not persist your conversations on our servers after the response is produced. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat that isn't needed for a vehicle question.

Lawyer: confirm Verify the AI provider's retention and training terms for the specific API tier you use, and describe them accurately here. This is a common area of privacy-policy inaccuracy.

7. Location data

Find Help Nearby uses your device's location (with your permission) or a ZIP code you type. We use it only to run that search and show nearby results. We don't track your location in the background or build a location history.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your account and vehicle data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your associated data from our database. Some records may be retained where required by law (for example, payment and tax records).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you — you can export it anytime from your Account page;
  • Delete your account and data — also from your Account page;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent;
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise a right we don't yet offer in-app, contact us (Section 15).

10. How we protect it

We protect your data with encrypted connections, authentication on every request that touches your data, strict per-user access scoping, rate limiting, and access controls on our infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and to fix issues promptly.

11. Cookies & local storage

AuraLink uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences (such as your theme and plan). We use only what's needed to run the Service; we don't use third-party advertising trackers.

12. Children

AuraLink is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.
